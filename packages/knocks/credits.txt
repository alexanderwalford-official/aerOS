FOUR KNOCKS - where everything in it comes from

Everything in this game was made for it, from nothing, by the scripts below, which are in
the AerOS repository and can be run again to make exactly the same files. No sound, picture,
model, music or words were recorded, photographed, downloaded or copied from anywhere else, and
nothing here comes from any other game, film or book.

All of it is under the same licence as AerOS itself: the Apache License 2.0 (LICENSE.md in the
repository).

  The game (knocks.aer)             written in AerLang for AerOS

  Pictures of walls, floors and     tools/make_knocks_textures.py
  ceilings (26 .bmp files)          noise, blocks, bevels and a small hand-drawn 5 x 7 alphabet,
                                    worked out pixel by pixel

  The thing, the furniture and      tools/make_knocks_sprites.py
  the close-up (beast.bmp,          3D models built from "signed distance functions" (capsules,
  lunge.bmp, props.bmp, scare.bmp)  spheres, boxes, blended and cut), posed for each frame of
                                    the walk, rendered by ray marching from 5 sides

  Every sound, the music and the    tools/make_knocks_sounds.py
  voices (37 .wav files)            filtered noise, resonators, sine and sawtooth waves, simple
                                    reverb, and formant synthesis for the voices (the words
                                    are shown as subtitles; the voices only sound like speech
                                    down a bad line)

  The story and every word of it    written for this game

The letters on the screen are AerOS's own 8 x 16 font.
